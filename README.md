# NomadMikeOS
My first attempt to build the framework, scripts, and objects for a local model.

# Purpose
Localized LLMs are growing in popularity due to corporate-owned models being trained, formed, and guardrailed for legal compliance and safety.  While I regularly use ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude to make most of workflows, research, and work product more efficient, I do have projects and work product isn't appropriate for one of those three models, or handles edge cases that are either unorthodox or uncommon.  Among my edge cases are health monitoring, complex document processing, handling sensitive research, and work product that the aforemntioned models may be hesistant to work with out of the fear of being sued.  Additionally, the aforementioned models have to comply with regulations and policies spanning the globe; I only have to worry about what applies to me.
